# Placement Policy 2026-27

Kaveri Ridge Institute of Technology (KRIT), Training and Placement Cell. This policy applies to all final-year B.Tech students of the 2027 graduating batch who register for campus placements. (Fictional sample document.)

## 1. Registration

Every student who wants to take part in campus placements must register with the Training and Placement Cell before 31 August 2026. Registration is free. A student who does not register cannot sit for any campus drive during the season. Registration is done on the placement portal and must be confirmed by the department placement coordinator.

## 2. Basic eligibility to register

A student may register for placements if all of the following are true:

- The aggregate CGPA up to the sixth semester is 6.0 or above.
- The student has no more than two active backlogs at the time of registration.
- The student has no disciplinary action pending.

Each company sets its own criteria on top of this. Company criteria are always the stricter rule: meeting the college's basic eligibility does not make a student eligible for a company that asks for a higher CGPA or fewer backlogs.

## 3. Backlogs

An active backlog is a course the student has failed and has not yet cleared. A cleared backlog (history of arrears) is a course that was failed once and passed later. Unless a company says otherwise, company criteria refer to active backlogs. Students must report backlog status honestly; a wrong declaration leads to debarment under section 10.

## 4. Company categories

Companies visiting campus are placed in three categories by the cost to company (CTC) they offer:

- **Regular:** CTC below 6 LPA.
- **Standard:** CTC from 6 LPA up to but not including 10 LPA.
- **Dream:** CTC of 10 LPA and above.

Where a company offers a CTC range, the lower figure of the range decides the category.

## 5. One-offer policy

A student who receives an offer through campus placements is considered placed and cannot sit for further companies, with one exception.

**The dream exception.** A placed student may appear for at most one more company, and only if that company is in a higher category than the offer already held. A student with a Regular offer may attempt one Standard or one Dream company. A student with a Standard offer may attempt one Dream company. A student with a Dream offer is out of the process.

If the student gets the second offer, the first offer is released and the student must accept the second. A student cannot hold two offers. Offers received off campus are not counted under this policy, but students are expected to inform the cell.

## 6. Internships and pre-placement offers

A pre-placement offer (PPO) received from a summer internship is treated as a campus offer in the category of its CTC. A student who accepts a PPO falls under the one-offer policy from the date of acceptance. A student who declines a PPO in writing within 7 days can continue in the process as an unplaced student.

## 7. Attendance and training

- Pre-placement training sessions (aptitude, communication and technical) are compulsory. A minimum attendance of 75 percent in these sessions is required to sit for campus drives.
- Students with attendance below 75 percent may apply to the Placement Officer for condonation once, with a valid reason and proof.
- Regular class attendance rules of the institute continue to apply. Missing a class for a drive held on campus is treated as on-duty leave if the student's name is on the drive list.

## 8. Applying to a company

- Students apply through the placement portal before the company's deadline. Late applications are not accepted.
- Applying to a company is a commitment to attend its process. A student who has applied may withdraw up to 24 hours before the first round.
- A student who is shortlisted must attend every round until selected or rejected.

## 9. Process stages

A typical campus drive has these stages:

1. Pre-placement talk by the company.
2. Online assessment (aptitude, coding or both).
3. Group discussion, in some companies.
4. One or more technical interviews.
5. HR interview.
6. Result announcement and offer.

The company profile on the portal lists the exact rounds for each company. Results are announced only by the Placement Cell. Students must not contact the company directly about results.

## 10. No-shows and debarment

- A student who applies and then misses the process without withdrawing in time is debarred from the next two campus drives.
- A second no-show leads to debarment for the rest of the season.
- Giving false information about CGPA, backlogs or projects, or malpractice in any test, leads to debarment for the rest of the season and is reported to the Head of Department.

## 11. Accepting an offer

A student must accept or decline an offer on the portal within 3 working days of the result being announced. An offer that is not answered within 3 working days is treated as declined, and the student is then considered unplaced but loses the right to the dream exception for that season.

## 12. Documents to carry

For every drive, students must carry:

- Three printed copies of the resume, one page preferred and two pages at most.
- College identity card.
- Photocopies of 10th, 12th and all semester mark sheets.
- Two passport-size photographs.
- A government photo identity card.
- A no-objection certificate from the department, if the drive is held off campus.

## 13. Dress code and conduct

- Formal dress is compulsory for pre-placement talks and all interview rounds: a plain formal shirt and trousers or a formal salwar kameez or saree, with closed formal shoes.
- Jeans, T-shirts and sports shoes are not allowed in interview rounds.
- Students must be seated 15 minutes before the reporting time. Phones must be switched off inside the test hall and interview area.

## 14. Higher studies

A student planning higher studies may opt out of placements by informing the cell in writing before 30 September 2026. A student who opts out and later wants to return can rejoin once, but only for companies whose deadlines have not passed.

## 15. Help and grievances

Questions about this policy go to the department placement coordinator first. Unresolved issues can be sent to the Placement Officer at placements@krit.example, and are answered within 5 working days.
