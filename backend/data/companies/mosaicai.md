# Mosaic AI Works - Machine Learning Engineer

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

Mosaic AI Works is a 35-person startup that builds document-reading models for insurance claims: extracting fields from scanned forms and flagging suspicious claims.

## Role

Machine Learning Engineer. The first six months are an internship at a stipend of 40,000 rupees a month, followed by a full-time offer based on performance. The work covers data cleaning, training and evaluating models, and writing the Python services that serve them.

## Compensation

Full-time CTC is 9 LPA to 11 LPA after the internship. This is a Standard category company under the placement policy, because the lower figure of the range is below 10 LPA. Location is Bengaluru.

## Eligibility

- Branches: CSE and IT.
- Minimum CGPA: 8.0.
- Active backlogs: at most 1.
- Batch: 2027 graduates, available for a six-month internship from January 2027.

## Selection process

1. **Online Assessment (60 minutes).** Python, probability and statistics, and machine learning multiple-choice questions.
2. **ML Take-home Task (72 hours).** A small tabular dataset: build a classifier, report precision and recall, and explain the mistakes the model makes. The write-up matters as much as the score.
3. **Technical Interview (60 minutes).** Bias and variance, overfitting, evaluation metrics, how to split data properly, and NumPy and pandas exercises.
4. **Founder Round (30 minutes).** A conversation about a paper or project the candidate chooses.

## Skills they look for

Required: Python, machine learning fundamentals, NumPy, pandas, statistics. Good to have: PyTorch, SQL.

## Important dates

- Apply by: 20 December 2026.
- Online Assessment: 22 December 2026.
- Take-home task: 23 to 26 December 2026.
- Interviews: 29 December 2026.

## Notes

Conversion from internship to full-time last year was 7 out of 9 interns.
