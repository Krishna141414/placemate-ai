# Nimbusware Technologies - Software Engineer Trainee

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

Nimbusware Technologies builds billing and inventory software for small retail chains. The engineering team of about 180 people works from Bengaluru and Mysuru. Most products are Python services with a PostgreSQL database behind them.

## Role

Software Engineer Trainee. New joiners spend the first three months in a training programme on Python, SQL and the company's code review process, and then join a product team. Day-to-day work is writing backend features, fixing bugs and writing tests.

## Compensation

CTC is 6.5 LPA during the first year and is revised to 8.0 LPA after confirmation at 12 months. This is a Standard category company under the placement policy. Job location is Bengaluru or Mysuru.

## Eligibility

- Branches: CSE, IT and ECE.
- Minimum CGPA: 7.0.
- Active backlogs: at most 1 at the time of applying. The backlog must be cleared before joining.
- Batch: 2027 graduates.

## Selection process

1. **Online Assessment (90 minutes).** 20 aptitude questions, 10 SQL multiple-choice questions and 2 coding problems of easy to medium difficulty. Python, Java and C++ are allowed.
2. **Technical Interview (45 to 60 minutes).** Data structures (arrays, strings, hash maps, trees), one SQL query written on paper, and a walk through one project from the resume. Interviewers ask candidates to explain their own code.
3. **HR Interview (20 minutes).** Relocation, the service agreement and general fit.

## Skills they look for

Required: Python, SQL, data structures. Good to have: Django, Git, REST API design.

## Important dates

- Apply by: 5 December 2026.
- Online Assessment: 9 December 2026.
- Interviews on campus: 12 December 2026.

## Notes

There is a service agreement of 18 months. Candidates who leave earlier pay back the training cost of 75,000 rupees.
