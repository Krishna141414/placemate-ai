# Quantaleaf Analytics - Data Analyst

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

Quantaleaf Analytics prepares sales and supply dashboards for consumer goods distributors. Analysts work with client data in SQL and deliver weekly reports. The company has around 90 employees in Hyderabad.

## Role

Data Analyst. The work is cleaning data, writing SQL, building dashboards and explaining numbers to clients on calls. Good spoken English matters because analysts present their own work.

## Compensation

CTC ranges from 5.5 LPA to 7.0 LPA depending on the interview rating. Because the lower figure is below 6 LPA, this is a Regular category company under the placement policy. Location is Hyderabad.

## Eligibility

- Branches: CSE, IT, ECE, EEE and MECH.
- Minimum CGPA: 6.5.
- Active backlogs: at most 1.
- Batch: 2027 graduates.

## Selection process

1. **Aptitude Test (60 minutes).** Quantitative aptitude, data interpretation and logical reasoning. No coding.
2. **SQL Case Round (45 minutes).** Candidates get three tables from a made-up retail store and write five queries: joins, group by, a window function and one query that needs a subquery.
3. **Technical Interview (30 minutes).** Basic statistics (mean, median, standard deviation, correlation), Excel functions such as VLOOKUP and pivot tables, and one guesstimate question.
4. **HR Interview (15 minutes).**

## Skills they look for

Required: SQL, Excel, Python, statistics. Good to have: Power BI, pandas.

## Important dates

- Apply by: 28 November 2026.
- Aptitude Test and SQL Case Round: 2 December 2026.
- Interviews: 3 December 2026.

## Notes

No service agreement. Non-CS branches are welcome; about a third of last year's hires were from ECE and MECH.
