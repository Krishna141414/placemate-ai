# Tessera Consulting - Associate Analyst

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

Tessera Consulting is an IT services company that runs application support and testing projects for banks and insurers. It is the largest recruiter on campus by headcount and took 64 students last year.

## Role

Associate Analyst. New joiners go through a 10-week training programme in Thiruvananthapuram and are then assigned to a project in support, testing or development, depending on training scores.

## Compensation

CTC is 4.25 LPA, fixed for all hires. This is a Regular category company under the placement policy. Students placed here can still use the dream exception for one Standard or Dream company.

## Eligibility

- Branches: all B.Tech branches (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, MECH, CIVIL).
- Minimum CGPA: 6.0.
- Active backlogs: at most 2, to be cleared before the joining date.
- Batch: 2027 graduates.

## Selection process

1. **Aptitude and Verbal Test (90 minutes).** Quantitative aptitude (percentages, time and work, profit and loss), logical reasoning and English grammar and comprehension. There is sectional cut-off but no negative marking.
2. **Communication Assessment (20 minutes).** A recorded speaking test: read a passage aloud, then speak for one minute on a given topic.
3. **Technical and HR Interview (25 minutes, combined).** Basics of one programming language, simple SQL queries, the final-year project, and willingness to relocate and work in any technology.

## Skills they look for

Required: aptitude, communication, basic SQL. Good to have: Excel, Java basics.

## Important dates

- Apply by: 30 November 2026.
- Tests: 4 December 2026.
- Interviews: 5 and 6 December 2026.

## Notes

A two-year service agreement applies. Joining dates are given in batches between July and December 2027.
