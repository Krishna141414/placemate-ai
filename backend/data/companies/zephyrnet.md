# ZephyrNet Security - Security Analyst Trainee

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

ZephyrNet Security runs a security operations centre for banks and hospitals. Analysts watch alerts around the clock and investigate suspicious activity.

## Role

Security Analyst Trainee in the monitoring team. The job is triaging alerts, reading logs, writing short incident reports and learning to hunt for threats. Work is in rotational shifts.

## Compensation

CTC is 8 LPA to 9.5 LPA. This is a Standard category company under the placement policy. Location is Hyderabad.

## Eligibility

- Branches: CSE and IT.
- Minimum CGPA: 7.5.
- Active backlogs: at most 1.
- Batch: **2026 graduates only.** This is an immediate-joining drive for students who have already passed out. Students of the 2027 batch cannot apply; the company plans a separate drive for them in March 2027.

## Selection process

1. **Online Assessment (60 minutes).** Networking, Linux and web security multiple-choice questions.
2. **Practical Lab Round (90 minutes).** A capture-the-flag style exercise: find three flags in a deliberately vulnerable web application.
3. **Technical Interview (45 minutes).** The OWASP Top 10, how HTTPS works, common ports, and reading a sample log to spot an attack.
4. **HR Interview (15 minutes).**

## Skills they look for

Required: networking, Linux, Python, web security basics. Good to have: OWASP Top 10 knowledge, Bash.

- Interviews: 26 November 2026.
