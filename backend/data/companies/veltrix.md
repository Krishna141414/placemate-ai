# Veltrix Systems - Software Development Engineer I

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

Veltrix Systems makes logistics routing software used by courier companies. The product handles several million shipment updates a day, so the engineering culture is centred on performance and correctness.

## Role

Software Development Engineer I in a backend team. Engineers own services written in Java, take part in on-call rotations after six months and are expected to write design documents for anything non-trivial.

## Compensation

CTC is 12 LPA to 14 LPA, including a joining bonus of 1 LPA. This is a Dream category company under the placement policy. Location is Pune.

## Eligibility

- Branches: CSE and IT only.
- Minimum CGPA: 8.5.
- Active backlogs: none allowed. Candidates must also have no history of more than one cleared backlog.
- Batch: 2027 graduates.

## Selection process

1. **Online Coding Test (120 minutes).** Three problems: one easy, one medium and one hard. Typical topics are graphs, dynamic programming and binary search on the answer.
2. **Technical Interview 1 (60 minutes).** Two data structure and algorithm problems solved on a shared editor, with time and space complexity.
3. **Technical Interview 2 (60 minutes).** Object-oriented design of a small system such as a parking lot or a rate limiter, plus DBMS questions on indexing, transactions and normalisation.
4. **Hiring Manager Round (45 minutes).** Project deep-dive, basic system design and behavioural questions.

## Skills they look for

Required: Java, data structures, algorithms, object-oriented programming, DBMS. Good to have: system design basics, Spring Boot.

## Important dates

- Apply by: 25 November 2026.
- Online Coding Test: 27 November 2026.
- Interviews: 30 November 2026.

## Notes

The cut-off in the coding test is usually two fully solved problems. There is no service agreement.
