# Halcyon Embedded - Embedded Engineer

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

Halcyon Embedded designs controller boards and firmware for home appliances such as washing machines and water purifiers. The design centre is in Coimbatore.

## Role

Embedded Engineer. The work is writing firmware in C for ARM microcontrollers, bringing up new boards with the hardware team and testing on the bench with an oscilloscope and logic analyser.

## Compensation

CTC is 5 LPA to 6.5 LPA. Because the lower figure is below 6 LPA, this is a Regular category company under the placement policy.

## Eligibility

- Branches: ECE and EEE only.
- Minimum CGPA: 7.0.
- Active backlogs: at most 1.
- Batch: 2027 graduates.

## Selection process

1. **Written Technical Test (60 minutes).** C programming (pointers, bit manipulation, structures), digital electronics and basic analog circuits. The test is on paper.
2. **Technical Interview (45 minutes).** Writing a function to set and clear bits in a register, explaining interrupts and timers, the difference between I2C and SPI, and a discussion of a microcontroller project.
3. **HR Interview (15 minutes).**

## Skills they look for

Required: C, embedded C, microcontrollers, digital electronics. Good to have: RTOS concepts, I2C and SPI protocols.

## Important dates

- Apply by: 15 December 2026.
- Written Technical Test: 18 December 2026.
- Interviews: 19 December 2026.

## Notes

Candidates should bring their project hardware or photos of it if possible. A one-year service agreement applies.
