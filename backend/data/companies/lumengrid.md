# LumenGrid Energy - Software Engineer (IoT)

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

LumenGrid Energy builds smart electricity meters and the software that collects their readings. Around two million meters report to its platform every 15 minutes.

## Role

Software Engineer (IoT). Engineers work on either meter-side software in C++ or the data collection services in Python. New joiners choose a side after a month of rotation.

## Compensation

CTC is 6.5 LPA to 8 LPA. This is a Standard category company under the placement policy. Location is Bengaluru.

## Eligibility

- Branches: CSE, ECE and EEE.
- Minimum CGPA: 7.0.
- Active backlogs: none allowed at the time of applying. Students with even one active backlog are not shortlisted.
- Batch: 2027 graduates.

## Selection process

1. **Online Assessment (90 minutes).** Two coding problems in C++ or Python and 20 questions on operating systems, networking and basic electronics.
2. **Technical Interview (45 minutes).** Pointers and memory in C++, Python data structures, a simple SQL query, and how a device should behave when the network drops.
3. **Project Presentation (20 minutes).** Each candidate presents one project for 10 minutes and answers questions for 10 minutes. Hardware projects are welcome.
4. **HR Interview (15 minutes).**

## Skills they look for

Required: C++, Python, IoT fundamentals, SQL. Good to have: MQTT, Linux.

## Important dates

- Apply by: 18 December 2026.
- Online Assessment: 21 December 2026.
- Interviews and presentations: 23 December 2026.

## Notes

No service agreement. The company conducts a pre-placement talk on 20 December 2026 that all applicants must attend.
