# Ferrowind Motors - Graduate Engineer Trainee

(Fictional sample company.)

## About the company

Ferrowind Motors manufactures gearboxes and drive shafts for tractors and small commercial vehicles. It runs two plants, in Hosur and Nashik.

## Role

Graduate Engineer Trainee. Trainees rotate through production, quality and design for one year and are then placed in one department. Shift work is part of the first year.

## Compensation

CTC is 4.5 LPA during training and 5.5 LPA after confirmation. This is a Regular category company under the placement policy. Subsidised accommodation is available near the Hosur plant.

## Eligibility

- Branches: MECH, EEE and CIVIL. Computer science and IT students are not eligible for this role.
- Minimum CGPA: 6.0.
- Active backlogs: at most 2, to be cleared before joining.
- Batch: 2027 graduates.

## Selection process

1. **Aptitude and Technical Test (75 minutes).** Quantitative aptitude plus branch questions on thermodynamics, strength of materials and manufacturing processes.
2. **Group Discussion (20 minutes).** Groups of eight; topics are usually about industry or current affairs.
3. **Technical Interview (30 minutes).** Engineering drawing, reading a simple AutoCAD drawing, basics of SolidWorks modelling and questions on the final-year project.
4. **HR Interview (15 minutes).** Willingness to work in shifts and to relocate.

## Skills they look for

Required: AutoCAD, SolidWorks, thermodynamics, manufacturing processes. Good to have: ANSYS, Six Sigma awareness.

## Important dates

- Apply by: 10 December 2026.
- Test and Group Discussion: 14 December 2026.
- Interviews: 15 December 2026.

## Notes

A two-year service bond applies. A medical fitness test is done before joining.
