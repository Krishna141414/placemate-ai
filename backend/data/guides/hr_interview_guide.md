# HR Interview Preparation Guide

(Fictional sample guide written for the KRIT placement cell.)

## What the HR round checks

By the HR round the company already believes you can do the job. This round checks three things: can you communicate clearly, will you accept the offer and stay, and are there any surprises such as relocation problems or a misunderstanding about the role.

## Questions you will almost certainly get

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why do you want to join this company?
3. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
4. Tell me about a time you worked in a team and something went wrong.
5. Where do you see yourself in five years?
6. Are you willing to relocate or work in shifts?
7. Are you comfortable with the service agreement?
8. Do you have any questions for us?

## The STAR method

For any "tell me about a time" question, answer in four parts:

- **Situation:** the context, in one sentence.
- **Task:** what you were responsible for.
- **Action:** what you did, in the most detail.
- **Result:** what happened, with a number if you have one.

Prepare four stories in advance: a team conflict, a failure, a time you led something, and a time you learned something quickly. Most behavioural questions can be answered with one of these four.

## "Tell me about yourself"

Keep it under 90 seconds. Start with your branch and college, then one or two projects or an internship that relate to the role, then why you are interested in this role. Do not recite your resume and do not start from your school.

## Weaknesses

Name a real but manageable weakness and what you are doing about it. "I used to avoid speaking in groups, so I started presenting in the coding club every month" works. "I am a perfectionist" does not.

## Service agreements and relocation

Read the company profile before the interview. If the company has a service agreement or shift work, decide your answer in advance. Saying yes in the interview and declining the offer later can lead to action under the placement policy.

## Questions to ask them

Ask one or two: what the first three months look like, how new joiners are trained, or how teams are assigned. Do not ask about salary revisions or leave in this round.

## Conduct

- Follow the dress code in the placement policy: formal wear and formal shoes.
- Reach 15 minutes early and keep your phone switched off.
- Carry three copies of your resume and your documents.
- Thank the interviewer. Do not ask for the result; results come only from the placement cell.
