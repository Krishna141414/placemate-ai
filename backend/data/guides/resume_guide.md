# Resume Writing Guide

(Fictional sample guide written for the KRIT placement cell.)

## Length and format

- One page is preferred for campus placements. Two pages is the maximum.
- Use one plain font, sizes 10 to 12 for the body, with clear section headings.
- Save and share it as a PDF named with your name and branch.
- No photo, no date of birth, no father's name and no full home address. City and state are enough.

## Section order for a fresher

1. Name and contact details (phone, email, GitHub or LinkedIn link).
2. Education, with CGPA up to the latest semester.
3. Skills, grouped: languages, frameworks, databases, tools.
4. Projects (two to four).
5. Internships or work experience.
6. Achievements and activities.

## Writing project points

Each project gets a title, the technologies used, and two or three points. Each point should say what you did and what came of it.

Weak: "Worked on a website for the hostel."

Strong: "Built a Django web app for hostel mess feedback used by 300 students; wrote the SQL reports that wardens review every week."

Start each point with a verb such as built, designed, wrote, tested or reduced. Add a number where you honestly can: users, records, response time or percentage improvement.

## Skills section

List only skills you can answer questions on. Interviewers pick a skill from your resume and go deep. A short honest list beats a long one. Match the words in the job description when they are true for you: if the company asks for "REST API" and you have built one, use that phrase.

## Tailoring for a company

Before applying, read the company profile and compare its required skills with your resume. Move the most relevant project to the top, and make sure the required skills you have are visible in the skills section and in at least one project point. Do not add a skill you do not have.

## Common mistakes

- Tutorial projects that you cannot explain.
- Spelling and grammar errors. Ask two friends to proofread.
- Listing soft skills such as "hard working" without evidence.
- Different CGPA on the resume and on the mark sheet. This is treated as false information under the placement policy.
- Objective statements that could apply to any company.

## Before every drive

Print three copies, as the placement policy requires, and reread your own resume. Be ready to talk for two minutes about every line on it.
