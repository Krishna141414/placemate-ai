# Tessera Consulting interview experience - Manoj C. (CIVIL, 2026 batch, selected)

(Fictional sample write-up.)

I am from Civil and had two active backlogs when I applied. Tessera allows up to two, as long as you clear them before joining. I cleared both in the next supplementary exam.

## Aptitude and Verbal Test

I prepared for three weeks using the placement cell's training sessions. Attending those sessions matters for another reason: the policy needs 75 percent attendance in pre-placement training to sit for drives, and the cell does check the register.

The quantitative section was manageable. I struggled with the reading passage because of time, so I answered the grammar questions first.

## Communication Assessment

My topic was "my favourite place in my home town". I prepared a simple structure for any topic: one line to introduce it, two points with an example each, and one closing line.

## Technical and HR Interview

Since I am from Civil, the interviewer did not ask programming in depth. He asked what a database is, what SQL is used for, and whether I had written any code. I showed a small C program from first year and explained it. Then he asked about my project on rainwater harvesting and why I wanted to move to IT.

He was clear that the role could be in application support with shifts. I said yes.

## Tips

- Non-circuit branches can get through. Be honest about what you know.
- Clear your backlogs before the joining date, or the offer is withdrawn.
- Carry all mark sheets. Document verification is done on the same day.
