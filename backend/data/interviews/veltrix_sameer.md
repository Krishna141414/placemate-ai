# Veltrix Systems interview experience - Sameer D. (IT, 2026 batch, not selected)

(Fictional sample write-up.)

I reached Technical Interview 2 and was rejected there.

## Online Coding Test

I solved two of the three problems. The hard problem was dynamic programming on intervals and almost nobody finished it, so do not panic if you cannot.

## Technical Interview 1

Went well. Questions were to merge k sorted lists and to find the longest increasing subsequence. I gave the O(n squared) solution first and then the O(n log n) one with binary search.

## Technical Interview 2

I was asked to design a rate limiter as classes. I jumped straight into code without asking what kind of limit was wanted (per user, per IP, fixed window or sliding window). The interviewer had to stop me and ask those questions himself. In DBMS he asked about normalisation up to 3NF with an example, and what happens when two transactions update the same row. My answers were vague.

## What I learned

- In design rounds, the first five minutes should be questions, not code.
- Veltrix expects DBMS depth: transactions, locking and indexing, not just definitions.
- Their minimum CGPA of 8.5 and the no-backlog rule are checked strictly at the document verification stage. One of my friends was removed from the list because his mark sheet showed an active backlog.
