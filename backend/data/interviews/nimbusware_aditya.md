# Nimbusware Technologies interview experience - Aditya M. (ECE, 2026 batch, not selected)

(Fictional sample write-up.)

I am writing this so juniors from ECE know what to expect. I cleared the Online Assessment but was rejected in the Technical Interview.

## Online Assessment

Being from ECE, the aptitude section was my strength. In coding I solved one problem fully (two-sum using a hash map) and half of the second.

## Technical Interview

This is where it went wrong. The interviewer asked about the Python project on my resume, a weather dashboard I had mostly followed from a tutorial. He asked why I chose a particular library and how I would add caching, and I could not answer. He then asked me to write a SQL query joining three tables, and I mixed up LEFT JOIN and INNER JOIN.

The data structure question was to detect a cycle in a linked list. I knew the slow and fast pointer idea but could not code it cleanly.

## What I would do differently

- Do not put a tutorial project on your resume unless you have changed it enough to explain every decision.
- ECE students are judged on the same coding and SQL bar as CSE. Start SQL practice at least a month earlier.
- Do mock interviews. I knew the answers at my desk but froze when speaking.

I was placed later in another company after two more weeks of practice, so one rejection is not the end.
