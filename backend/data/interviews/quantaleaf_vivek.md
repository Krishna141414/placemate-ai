# Quantaleaf Analytics interview experience - Vivek T. (MECH, 2026 batch, selected)

(Fictional sample write-up.)

I am from Mechanical and had no coding background beyond first-year C. I learned SQL and Excel in about six weeks for this drive.

## Preparation

I did two things every day: 30 aptitude questions and 5 SQL problems. For Excel I built a small dashboard from our college fest budget, which later became a talking point in the interview.

## SQL Case Round

Mine had employees, departments and attendance tables. The queries were about average attendance per department, employees absent more than three days in a row, and the department with the highest attendance each month. The consecutive-days question was the hardest. I solved it with LAG, comparing each date with the previous one.

## Technical Interview

The interviewer asked me to explain a pivot table to someone who had never used Excel. Then basic Python: read a CSV with pandas and find the average of a column by group. Statistics questions were on percentiles and outliers.

He also asked why a Mechanical student wanted to be an analyst. I talked about a quality control project where I had analysed defect data, and that answer went well.

## Tips

- Non-CS students: connect your branch projects to data. It is the best answer to "why analytics".
- Learn pandas basics even though the main test is SQL.
- The CTC depends on your interview rating, so the technical round matters even after you are sure of selection.
