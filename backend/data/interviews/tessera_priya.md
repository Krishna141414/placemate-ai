# Tessera Consulting interview experience - Priya N. (EEE, 2026 batch, selected)

(Fictional sample write-up.)

## Aptitude and Verbal Test

Ninety minutes with a cut-off in each section, so you cannot skip one. Quantitative had percentages, profit and loss, time and work, and simple probability. Reasoning had seating arrangements and number series. Verbal had a reading passage, sentence correction and fill in the blanks. There is no negative marking, so attempt everything.

Around 420 students wrote the test and about 200 cleared it.

## Communication Assessment

This is a recorded test on a computer with a headset. First you read a short passage aloud. Then you get a topic and one minute to speak. Mine was "a skill I learned outside the classroom". Speak slowly and finish your sentences. Many people lost marks by speaking too fast or stopping after 20 seconds.

## Technical and HR Interview

One panel member, 25 minutes. Technical questions were basic: what is a class and an object, the difference between a primary key and a foreign key, write a query to get the names of students with marks above 80, and explain my final-year project in simple words.

HR questions: are you willing to relocate anywhere in India, are you fine with working in testing or support, and are you aware of the two-year service agreement.

## Tips

- This drive is won in the aptitude test. Practise with a timer.
- For the speaking test, practise one-minute talks with a friend.
- Tessera is a Regular category company, so you can still use the dream exception later. Several of us took this offer as a safety net and then attempted one higher company.
