# Quantaleaf Analytics interview experience - Meghana P. (ECE, 2026 batch, selected)

(Fictional sample write-up.)

## Aptitude Test

Sixty minutes, no coding. Data interpretation took the most time: two sets of bar charts and tables with five questions each. Do the reasoning puzzles first, they are quicker.

## SQL Case Round

We were given three tables: stores, products and daily_sales. The five questions were:

1. Total sales per store for last month.
2. Top three products by revenue in each city.
3. Stores with no sales in the last seven days.
4. Month-on-month growth for each store.
5. Products that were sold in every store.

Question 2 needs a window function (RANK or ROW_NUMBER with PARTITION BY). Question 3 needs a LEFT JOIN with a NULL check or NOT EXISTS. I could not finish question 5 but explained my idea and that was accepted.

## Technical Interview

Statistics basics: when to use the median instead of the mean, what standard deviation tells you, and what correlation does not tell you. In Excel they asked about VLOOKUP, pivot tables and removing duplicates. The guesstimate was "how many cups of tea are sold in our city in a day?". They want to hear your assumptions, not a correct number.

## HR Interview

Why analytics, and whether an ECE student would be comfortable not doing core electronics.

## Tips

- Window functions are a must.
- Speak clearly. Analysts present to clients, and they check communication in every round.
