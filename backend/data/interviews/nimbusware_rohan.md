# Nimbusware Technologies interview experience - Rohan K. (CSE, 2026 batch, selected)

(Fictional sample write-up.)

## Online Assessment

The test was 90 minutes. Aptitude was easy if you had practised time and work and percentages. The SQL section had ten multiple-choice questions, mostly about joins and GROUP BY with HAVING. The two coding problems were: find the first non-repeating character in a string, and merge overlapping intervals. I solved both in Python. About 45 of 160 students were shortlisted.

## Technical Interview

It lasted about 50 minutes. The interviewer opened my resume and asked me to explain my Django project from start to finish: the models, why I chose them and how a request moves through the app. Then two coding questions on paper: reverse a linked list, and check if two strings are anagrams using a dictionary. He asked for the time complexity of each.

The SQL question was to write a query for the second highest salary in each department. I first wrote it with a subquery, and he asked if I could do it with a window function.

## HR Interview

Short. Questions were about relocating to Mysuru, the 18-month service agreement and why I wanted a product company.

## Tips

- Know every line of your own project. They do not ask textbook theory, they ask about what you built.
- Practise writing SQL by hand. There is no editor in the interview.
- Say your approach aloud before you write code.
